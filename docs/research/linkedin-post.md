What evidence do you need before accepting a change produced by an AI agent?

I am sharing a design I have been developing: an agentic SDLC framework with separated roles, explicit handoffs and claim verification.

The goal is to make each delivery traceable: who defined its scope, which artifact was reviewed and what evidence supports approval.

The paper explains the architecture and examines the 1.7.0 package. It includes an evidence-flow diagram and a breakdown of the 23 declared gates. It also identifies the next engineering steps: executable gate checks, verified isolation between producer and reviewer, and measurement of total cost, including human review.

I am not yet reporting measured improvements in accuracy or savings. The paper includes an evaluation protocol to test whether additional verification justifies its cost.

For teams bringing agents into software delivery: how do you verify an agent's claims before accepting its work?
